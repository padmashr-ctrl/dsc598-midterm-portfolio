About me

I design where systems meet feeling.

I'm Shreya Ramanan, an interdisciplinary designer exploring how images, spaces and interfaces shape what people notice, trust and remember.

I began this course interested in what AI could make. I am leaving it more interested in what a designer must decide: the rule behind an interaction, the evidence behind a claim, the material that carries an emotion and the moment a system should explain itself.

My work moves between visual design and code. Sometimes the outcome is a quiet album cover or a 3D room. Sometimes it is a browser game that changes the evidence around you. Across all of them, I use AI to generate possibilities and support implementation, while keeping concept, art direction, editing and accountability in my hands.

My working method

Define · Measure · Refine · Audit

A method developed across the semester, from the first prompt study to the final interactive work.

01

Define

Turn a mood or question into a clear experience and a few observable choices.

02

Measure

Give the important decisions values—placement, scale, colour, timing—so they can be checked.

03

Refine

Change one variable at a time, test it with people and keep only what strengthens the concept.

04

Audit

Look for what the system changed without permission and disclose how the work was made.

A few more things

What I care about.

Material still matters.Craft

The cross-stitch, linen, light and room proportions are not decoration. They are how an idea becomes felt instead of merely explained.

Interaction should reveal its rule.Experience

I like systems that let a visitor discover their logic through consequence: a line drifts, a memory disappears, a stitch comes back changed.

AI use should be legible.Responsibility

Every case study separates what a tool generated or implemented from the concept, selection, testing and editorial decisions I made.

I make work that is careful enough to question the system and playful enough to invite someone in.

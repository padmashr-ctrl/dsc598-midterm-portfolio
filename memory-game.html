      FINAL CUT · RESPONSE TO CRITIQUE AND PLAYTESTING

      The doubt is now something you play.

      Playtesters asked for two opposite things: make the rewrites easier to notice, and make them subtle enough to doubt. The final cut does both. Rewrites begin loud, soften, then go silent, and a new audit stage asks you to find them before the memory is filed.

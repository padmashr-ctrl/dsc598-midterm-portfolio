Statement

I treat AI as a material that needs a brief, a measurement, and an audit—never as a shortcut around a decision.

Across the semester the same lesson kept returning: a model does exactly what a vague instruction allows, so the design work is in making intentions specific enough to check. The album cover only worked once its mood became measurements. The studio held together once its colours became numbers. The game goes one step further and makes the checking itself the experience—asking players to notice what a confident system quietly changed.
